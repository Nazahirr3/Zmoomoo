Account privacy

Accounts are optional. You can play as a guest without signing in.

Information stored

When you sign in with Discord, the server stores your Discord account ID, your chosen player name, account creation and last sign-in times, and game statistics (kills, deaths, damage, healing, gathered resources, animal and boss kills, best score and kills, lives, and play time). It also stores chat mute offense counts and mute times so those punishments persist between sessions.

Login sessions are stored as hashes of random session tokens. The Discord access token and email address are not stored. The browser receives an HttpOnly session cookie.

Deletion

Use Delete account in the account panel to remove your account, sessions, statistics, and saved chat punishments from this server.

Storage

Account data is kept in the server operator's SQLite database. The operator is responsible for securing and backing it up.
